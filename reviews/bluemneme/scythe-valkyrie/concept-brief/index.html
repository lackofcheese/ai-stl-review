Pose and composition

Jake supplied this image as the primary composition direction: a poised, statuesque contrapposto with a long vertical S-curve, a steep two-handed scythe diagonal, an oversized crescent sweeping low behind the legs, and asymmetric high/low wing framing. That silhouette, balance, and swirl are what transfer.

What does not transfer: the source character and costume, its unclear merged hands, floating wing roots, fragile openwork, narrow high-heel contact, and absent support — those are source ambiguities, not requested features. The operative pose specification (explicit grips, blade clearance, mounting solution, and the retained secondary pose options) lives in the prose spec.

Scythe silhouette

Directional weapon-shape reference only. Preserve the broad crescent read and elegant techno-gothic character, then simplify it into thick, solid, printable geometry. Do not transfer source logos, padding, exact ornament, thin spikes, or fragile cut-outs.

Wing architecture and palette

This is the strongest wing reference: dark mechanical wing masses and a silver/black/red color hierarchy. Jake's written direction and the pose art separately supply the broader skull/death theme; no literal skull is visible in this wing image. Derive a new wing assembly and armor language.

Body proportions — four product angles

Use this set only for rough slender adult feminine proportions and broad front/rear/three-quarter volume checks. The image named side is actually a rear three-quarter product angle, not an orthographic profile; the long dress also hides most leg anatomy.

Aerith's likeness, hair, dress, jacket, staff, boots, printed commercial-statue dimensions, branded text, and display base are not design references. Scythe Valkyrie's body armor must be original and leave room for solid wing roots, a readable two-handed scythe hold, and print-safe foot/support contacts.

Face direction

High-level facial direction: mature East Asian features, a calm direct gaze, and a softly angular oval face. Translate that broad direction into a distinct original face; this is not an exact celebrity-likeness target.

Optional hood

This remains undecided. If selected, use only the deep face-framing hood and short shoulder-mantle construction as inspiration. Do not transfer the source costume, emblem, jewelry, glowing hands, belt, skirt, or scenery.
