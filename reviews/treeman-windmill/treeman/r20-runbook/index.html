How the round is judged

  • Source identity: both late R18 STLs and both R19 reference STLs must match the SHA-256 values pinned in the technical plan.
  • Placement contract: each late output is uniformly scaled to 50.00 mm overall Z height, centered in X/Y and moved so minimum Z is zero.
  • Geometry preservation: the operation may not delete the known microscopic shards in either late source. Face/component counts, winding, and main-shell topology must match the corresponding source; an independently derived source-to-output affine comparison must stay within 0.0001 mm.
  • Mechanical evidence: record overall and main-shell topology, dimensions, volume, surface roughness, and the 0.28 mm-pitch thin-connection probe. A1 M2 begins with one watertight 1,283,480-face main shell plus one one-face shard; A2 M8 begins with one watertight 1,203,674-face main shell plus eight-face and one-face shards. Those facts must be visible rather than silently cleaned.
  • Compass evidence: render every finalist at azimuths 270°, 315°, 0°, 45°, 90°, 135°, 180° and 225° at the same elevation and framing. These are the front, front-right, right, back-right, back, back-left, left and front-left steps in the established model convention.
  • Human comparison: Babs and Dimitri rotate the four exact meshes and weigh visual preference against topology and thin-feature risk. Static evidence is a pre-filter, not a substitute for live 3D inspection.

Independent final-scale checks

for CANDIDATE in a1-m2 a2-m8; do
  STL="$P/models/round-20/treeman-r20-${CANDIDATE}-clean-50mm.stl"
  python3 tools/mesh_validate.py --stl "$STL" --units mm \
    --label "treeman-r20-${CANDIDATE}-clean-50mm" \
    --out "$P/notes/r20-${CANDIDATE}-validation.json"
  python3 tools/mesh_stats.py --stl "$STL" --json
  python3 tools/mesh_roughness.py --stl "$STL" --bands \
    > "$P/notes/r20-${CANDIDATE}-roughness.txt"
  python3 tools/mesh_thin_parts.py --stl "$STL" --pitch 0.28 \
    --fragile-mm 0.8 > "$P/notes/r20-${CANDIDATE}-thin-parts.txt"
  python3 tools/mesh_thin_parts.py --stl "$STL" --pitch 0.28 --fragility \
    > "$P/notes/r20-${CANDIDATE}-fragility.txt"
done

Independently compare each output with its source under the placement report's declared affine transform. Fail if maximum coordinate error exceeds 0.0001 mm, or if face count, component-face counts, winding consistency, main-shell open edges or main-shell non-manifold edges change. The overall late meshes are not allowed to be called watertight while their preserved microscopic shards remain.
