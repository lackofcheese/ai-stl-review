Goal

Test whether Meshy's newer model rescues the ankh. Round 9 proved the current inputs reconstruct well overall — Dimitri's pick g14-m2 is close to printable — but the ankh pendant, Trumbert's key identity feature, came out badly on every one of the ten meshy-6 returns. This round re-runs the exact same four g14 views through meshy-7, twice (the standard stochastic-defect insurance), finishes both returns to 24 mm, and puts them beside the R10 baseline. If meshy-7 renders the ankh cleanly, we have our print candidate; if it fails the same way, the recorded conclusion is that the ankh must be fixed in the input art (thickened/enlarged in the turnaround, or deferred to paint stage) before any further 3D spend.

How the round is judged

FAST (as each mesh lands): immediate Drive push before anything else (--drive-sync); raw unit-neutral validation (mesh_validate.py --units raw); eight-view turnaround renders plus ankh/hands/ground zooms. Once both returns have fast evidence, the r11-panel is shared as PROVISIONAL — final-scale checks pending with interactive STL viewers.

SLOW (per mesh, refreshing the same panel to COMPLETE): the clean 24 mm derivative (weld; remove only independently proven microscopic debris; 24 mm figure height, XY-center, z=0 floor) and the full final-scale battery: dimensions/volume, watertightness/Euler/winding, surface roughness, mesh_thin_parts.py at the tight resin setting (pitch ~0.28, erode 1) with the ankh, scimitar blade/tip and ears as named risk regions, internal-void cross-sections, base-contact check, and clean lineage stats via mesh_stats.py. (Meshy's free Analyze Printability endpoint stays in docs/experiments-backlog.md as a future idea — no repository tool calls it yet, so it is NOT part of this round's evidence.) A fresh-context subagent then runs the standard per-feature COMPARATIVE audit of both candidates against the R10 baseline gold and the g14 source sheet — Dimitri's two pin-down features first: (1) the ankh (recognizable shape, complete top, not fused into the chest), (2) the shield straps (centered, correctly sized, rear strap under the arm, no sleeve overlap — the R9 fleet's strap-defect catalogue is in the per-artifact reviews.jsonl records) — then the full checklist (scimitar shape/grip, digit count, shield inside, sunburst, cloak, ears, feet/ground contact, detached or phantom geometry). Renders add a dedicated shield-strap zoom beside the ankh/hands/ground zooms.

Bounded cleanup only: bridging or deleting a meaningful component, cutting, boolean work, sculpting, or repositioning stops for a separately approved mesh-surgery round.

Human gate: Dimitri's live-3D three-way look (both meshy-7 candidates vs the R10 baseline) picks the print candidate — or rejects both and records the ankh as an input-art problem.
