You receive exactly TWO input images.

IMAGE 1 is the approved Tiger character concept and is the identity, anatomy,
costume, pose, and orientation reference. IMAGE 2 is a blank 2x2 turnaround
template. Return ONE completed image that fills the four template slots; keep
the divider geometry unchanged.

Create a strict rotational turntable of the exact same physical miniature in
the exact same planted pose, with an orthographic or very-long-lens camera at
the same height and scale in every panel. Reading order:

PANEL 1, top-left: exact FRONT view.
PANEL 2, top-right: exact TAIL-SIDE profile.
PANEL 3, bottom-left: exact BACK view.
PANEL 4, bottom-right: exact OPPOSITE-TAIL-SIDE profile.

Rotate the character by exactly 90 degrees between panels. Do not mirror,
redesign, repose, resize, or independently reinterpret a panel. Maintain one
coherent 3D body, wardrobe construction, limb placement, and character-side
orientation across all four views.

Render every surface as solid opaque monochrome neutral-grey matte clay: frog
skin, eyes, clothing, wraps, toggles, and sash alike. Use a plain mid-grey
studio background with soft even lighting. No colour, paint, gloss, sheen,
translucency, glow, text, labels, arrows, captions, base, floor disc, props, or
other figures.

Preserve the approved source exactly: an upright heavyweight anthropomorphic
frog; broad deep chest; thick limbs; bare frog head with dome eyes and wide
mouth; short-sleeved tangzhuang jacket with the source's THREE thick knotted
front toggles; broad wrapped sash; knee-length shorts; thick forearm and shin
wraps; bare webbed frog feet; and two empty four-digit webbed frog hands in the
same staggered tiger-claw guard. Both feet remain pressed flat along their full
length on one common ground plane in every view.

The source has exactly ONE short thick sash tail attached at the character's
RIGHT hip. In the front panel it appears on viewer-left. Keep that same single
physical tail on the same character side through every rotation. Never add a
second tail, swap sides between panels, detach it, float it, lengthen it into a
thin strip, or erase it from a view where it should be visible.

Panel-frame checks:

- FRONT: the raised hand is viewer-left, the low hand is viewer-right, and the
  one sash tail is viewer-left.
- TAIL-SIDE profile: the near arm is the raised arm, the far arm
  is low, and the single tail is on the near side.
- BACK: the raised hand is viewer-right, the low hand is viewer-left, and the
  tail is viewer-right. Show the true rear of the head and jacket; no face.
- OPPOSITE-TAIL-SIDE profile: the near arm is the low arm, the raised arm is
  far, and the tail is on the far side and may be partly occluded naturally.

The tiger is a martial-arts style, never the species. Keep unmistakable frog
anatomy: no feline face, fur, stripes, whiskers, cat ears, animal claws, talons,
nails, or pointed digits. Keep all hand and foot digits thick, rounded, webbed,
and printable. No headband, hat, hood, helmet, weapon, ball, footwear, loose
strap, floating cloth, or fragile spike.

How the round is judged

The sheet must show one coherent miniature, not four adjacent redesigns.

  • Rotation and pose: panels are true front, character-right profile, back, and character-left profile in reading order. Body proportions, hand heights, foot placement, and camera scale remain fixed. Both feet stay flat on one ground plane.
  • Orientation-bearing tail: exactly one short, thick, attached sash tail remains on Tiger's right hip. It is viewer-left in front, near in the right-side panel, viewer-right in back, and far/possibly naturally occluded in the left-side panel. A duplicated, floating, long, or side-swapping tail fails.
  • Identity and construction: heavyweight anthro frog, bare head, rounded webbed four-digit hands and feet, three chunky front toggles, tangzhuang, sash, shorts, and wraps. No feline traits, claws, headwear, weapon, ball, text, or base.
  • Material: every surface is opaque neutral-grey matte clay with no colour, gloss, translucency, or paint cues.

FAST evaluation crops the four rectangles by the deterministic divider grid and audits every panel together in a fresh context. SLOW evaluation submits both complete sheets to one two-wave blind_detect_round.py Batch run. The machines screen consistency; Jawaknight and Dimitri choose the keeper sheet or name a small panel repair. Human reconciliation remains authoritative when a machine judgment is stricter than the accepted design.
