Goal

Tiger is the first Toadal Kombat model, and his job is to set the frog standard every other frog on the team inherits. The single biggest open design decision is not his kit — it's how frog the frogs are: a real squat bullfrog wearing training clothes, a balanced frog-bodied martial artist, or a human-framed athlete with a frog head, webbed hands and feet. This round puts that dial in front of Jawaknight as three concrete renderings before any turnaround or 3D spend, the same body-recipe-first approach that just worked for Gorgon v2.

The choices on the table

Five decision-carrying models each render all three choices: the four ratified fresh-concept defaults — flash31, gptimage2, seedream45, grokq — plus riverflow25 as a named Supported specialist (Dimitri, 2026-08-03: it produced Gorgon v1's r4 gold concept from pure text-to-image, and the corrected policy note scopes its "expensive" reputation to input-heavy edits — live text-to-image is ~$0.15/image at 2K). Every kit, pose, and camera sentence is held byte-identical across the three prompts; only the named body-plan paragraph differs. Alongside them, flashlite31 (Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite Image, 1K) runs the same three prompts as this round's one named trial arm (Dimitri, 2026-08-03): its registered trial hypothesis — a cheap text-only selection arm for silhouette and pose — is exactly this round's task. Its outputs are admission evidence for the model policy and do NOT carry the body-plan decision.

No images are attached. Create a brand-new text-to-image concept from this description alone.

OUTPUT AND CAMERA
A photorealistic full-body studio character render designed specifically as production concept art for a 32 mm-scale resin fantasy-football miniature, in the visual language of a premium collectible sports sculpt. Show one figure only, straight-on FRONT view, centered on a plain neutral gray studio background with even soft lighting. Use a near-orthographic, very-long-lens studio view with minimal foreshortening so head, torso, both arms, both hands, legs and feet can be compared as a proportion sheet. The entire body and both feet are visible with space around them. This is character concept art, not a photograph of a toy: no base, pedestal, scenic ground, labels or presentation card.

FROG BODY PLAN — F1 TRUE BULLFROG
Build him as a NATURALISTIC SQUAT BULLFROG body plan, roughly TWO-AND-A-HALF to THREE heads tall. His huge wide head merges directly into his barrel torso with NO neck; his dome eyes sit right on TOP of his skull; his mouth line is very wide. Short immensely thick arms, a deep round body, and long powerfully folded digitigrade frog legs coiled beneath him, with oversized webbed hands and feet. He reads first as a real giant frog — a bullfrog heavyweight wearing training clothes — and only second as a humanoid athlete.

HELD-CONSTANT CHARACTER PROGRAM
TIGER is a FROG — a martial-arts fantasy-football lineman from an ancient kung-fu temple, rendered as the hero of a serious WUXIA epic: the disciplined gravitas, coiled power and quiet menace of a wuxia film lead, an ancient-Chinese martial-world tone carried in every element. Played absolutely straight — never cartoon-cute, never comedic, never a Ninja Turtles homage. He is the densest, most powerful member of his squad: wide, heavy, grounded strength with thick limbs and a deep chest. Smooth moss-green frog skin with a lighter throat and belly; no warts, no hair. His face is level and unsmiling, the wide frog mouth a firm closed line — never a grin, never googly eyes.

He is a master of TIGER-STYLE KUNG FU, the hard ferocious power style: every technique drives forward through open TIGER-CLAW hands, thick webbed digits hooked like striking claws. The tiger is his STYLE, not his species — he remains entirely a frog, with NO stripes, NO fur, NO whiskers and NO feline features anywhere.

His kit is minimal ancient training wear tailored to his frog anatomy: a knotted cloth HACHIMAKI headband across the brow below the eyes, tied at the back with a chunky knot and short tucked tails; a gi-style robe top with its sleeves TORN OFF at the shoulders, raw edges thickened, bare powerful arms; a broad cloth OBI belt wrapped high and tied at the back; simple cloth wraps on each forearm and each shin. His big four-digit hands and bare feet show natural connected webbing — no sandals, no boots. The kit is plain: NO emblem, number, logo or readable text, and he wears NO armor, pads or helmet of any kind. He carries NOTHING — no weapon, no ball, no prop; both hands are visibly empty.

POSE AND BASING
He stands in a deep, wide TIGER-STYLE BOW STANCE facing the camera with the poised stillness of a wuxia master a heartbeat before striking: weight sunk low through strongly bent knees, torso upright, chin level. BOTH bare webbed feet are FLAT on the same ground plane, planted wide and firmly grounded — no lifted foot, no tiptoe. Both arms present open tiger-claw hands toward the camera, one leading at shoulder height, one chambered lower near the obi, elbows bent, both arms wholly within the image plane. No limb crosses, merges into or hides behind the torso.

PRINT-SAFE FINISH
Treat every feature as geometry for a roughly 35 mm-tall resin figure: bold planes, thick connected forms, deep relief, no hairline detail. The headband knot and tails are chunky and pinned to the head; robe and obi hems are thickened and rest against the body; webbed digits stay connected; the throat and chin stay clear of clutter. No base, no text, no thin spike, no free-floating strap, no transparent material.

How the round is judged

Jawaknight makes the call, with Dimitri: which frog does he want sixteen of on his table? Two things worth weighing while looking:

  • Compare F1/F2/F3 within each model — the five models draw faces, cloth, and lighting very differently, so comparing an F1 from one model against an F3 from another compares house styles, not body plans.
  • The frog-first principle and the wuxia lean (our Ugni differentiators, brief §1/§10.6) pull toward F1/F2; visual kinship with his existing Hero Forge commando frogs pulls toward F3. That trade-off is his to make.

Machine checks run in two explicit lanes:

  • FAST: a fresh-context crop audit runs as each image lands, checking the held-constant program — hachimaki with short pinned tails, torn-off sleeves, high-wrapped obi, forearm/shin wraps, bare webbed feet with four connected webbed digits per hand and both hands empty in claw guard, both feet flat and planted, no weapon/ball/mon/number/text, smooth skin with no warts or hair, no tiger features (stripes, fur, whiskers, feline face — the style is tiger, the species is frog), and a serious wuxia non-cartoon tone. Once all FAST verdicts are logged, a private review may be shared as PROVISIONAL — slow checks pending so eye-review can start.
  • SLOW: after all 18 images land, one blind_detect_round.py manifest submits every spec-blind description in a single Gemini Batch wave and every dependent comparison in a second Batch wave. Completed records append to reviews.jsonl; every blind-vs-crop disagreement gets a mandatory human look. Dimitri then appends one authoritative verdict per artifact, after both machine records, whose summary starts RECONCILED — AGREE: when the screens agree or RECONCILED — RESOLVED: with the human resolution when they do not. The same panel is rebuilt as COMPLETE only after the canonical review log contains crop audits, successful records from that exact Batch run, and later reconciliation verdicts for all and only the exact 18 planned artifact ids. A partial detector run, a missing crop audit, or an unresolved disagreement remains incomplete even when the coordinator exits normally. The provisional panel cannot close the round, promote a keeper, or authorize downstream work.

The machines screen for misses; they do not pick the winner. The three flashlite31 trial renders get the identical screening; they are judged separately on the trial's own question — does a 1K Flash Lite render hold silhouette, pose, and the held-constant program well enough to serve future cheap selection bake-offs? — and never count toward the F1/F2/F3 pick.

# Coordinator completion is not sufficient: PARTIAL is a durable terminal
# state too. After the blind run lands, Dimitri appends one authoritative
# judgment record per artifact through review_log.py. Its final KEEPER/FIXABLE/
# REJECT call is the panel verdict, and its summary must begin with exactly one
# of these evidence markers:
#   RECONCILED — AGREE: <why the crop and blind judgment classes agree>
#   RECONCILED — RESOLVED: <what disagreed and Dimitri's resolution>
# Append that record only after reading both source records. The check below
# requires all and only the expected 18 crop audits, same-run blind records,
# and later human reconciliations before COMPLETE/public handoff. It derives
# each latest machine call's KEEPER/FIXABLE/REJECT class and rejects AGREE when
# those classes differ.
jq -e -s --arg run "$DETECT_RUN" --rawfile expected "$EXPECTED" '
  def call_class:
    (. // "" | match("^(KEEPER|FIXABLE|REJECT)")?.string);
  ($expected | split("\n") | map(select(length > 0)) | sort) as $want
  | to_entries as $rows
  | ([$rows[]
      | select(.value.reviewer | endswith("-crop-audit"))]) as $crops
  | ([$rows[]
      | select(.value.reviewer == "blind-detect"
               and .value.batch_run_id == $run)]) as $blinds
  | ([$rows[]
      | select(.value.reviewer == "dimitri"
               and ((.value.summary // "")
                    | test("^RECONCILED — (AGREE|RESOLVED): .+")))]) as $human
  | ([$crops[].value.artifact] | unique | sort) as $crop_ids
  | ([$blinds[].value.artifact] | unique | sort) as $blind_ids
  | ([$human[].value.artifact] | unique | sort) as $human_ids
  | ($want | length) == 18
    and $crop_ids == $want
    and $blind_ids == $want
    and $human_ids == $want
    and all($human[];
      (.value.call // "") | test("^(KEEPER|FIXABLE|REJECT)"))
    and all($want[]; . as $id |
      ([$crops[] | select(.value.artifact == $id)] | max_by(.key)) as $crop
      | ([$blinds[] | select(.value.artifact == $id)] | max_by(.key)) as $blind
      | ([$human[] | select(.value.artifact == $id)] | max_by(.key)) as $recon
      | ($crop.value.call | call_class) as $crop_class
      | ($blind.value.call | call_class) as $blind_class
      | $recon.key > $crop.key
        and $recon.key > $blind.key
        and (if $crop_class == $blind_class
             then ($recon.value.summary | startswith("RECONCILED — AGREE:"))
             else ($recon.value.summary | startswith("RECONCILED — RESOLVED:"))
             end))
' "$T/reviews.jsonl" > /dev/null
