Prompt — r2-anthro-tangzhuang.txt 5 kB

projects/martial-frogs/tiger/prompts/r2-anthro-tangzhuang.txt

No images are attached. Create a brand-new text-to-image concept from this description alone.

OUTPUT AND CAMERA
A photorealistic full-body studio character render designed specifically as production concept art for a 32 mm-scale resin fantasy-football miniature, in the visual language of a premium collectible sports sculpt. Show one figure only, straight-on FRONT view, centered on a plain neutral gray studio background with even soft lighting. Use a near-orthographic, very-long-lens studio view with minimal foreshortening so head, torso, both arms, both hands, legs and feet can be compared as a proportion sheet. The entire body and both feet are visible with space around them. This is character concept art, not a photograph of a toy: no base, pedestal, scenic ground, labels or presentation card.

FROG BODY PLAN — ANTHRO HEAVYWEIGHT
Build him as an ANTHRO ATHLETE FROG, roughly FIVE to FIVE-AND-A-HALF heads tall — an upright humanoid read. His frame, stance and limb proportions are those of a HEAVYWEIGHT human martial artist: wide shoulders, deep chest, thick powerful limbs — the densest, most powerful member of his squad, never lean or wiry. His head is an unmistakable frog head with dome eyes and a wide mouth on a short thick neck, and his hands and feet are big webbed frog hands and feet on long powerful frog legs. He reads first as a powerful humanoid athlete and second as a frog.

HELD-CONSTANT CHARACTER PROGRAM
TIGER is a FROG — a martial-arts fantasy-football lineman from an ancient kung-fu temple, rendered as the hero of a serious WUXIA epic: the disciplined gravitas, coiled power and quiet menace of a wuxia film lead, an ancient-Chinese martial-world tone carried in every element. Played absolutely straight — never cartoon-cute, never comedic, never a Ninja Turtles homage. Smooth moss-green frog skin with a lighter throat and belly; no warts, no hair. His head is COMPLETELY BARE: NO headband, NO hachimaki, NO hat, hood or helmet — nothing worn on the head at all. His face is level and unsmiling, the wide frog mouth a firm closed line — never a grin, never googly eyes.

He is a master of TIGER-STYLE KUNG FU, the hard ferocious power style: every technique drives forward through open TIGER-CLAW hands. "Tiger-claw" names the HAND SHAPE ONLY — his thick webbed digits are hooked like a striking claw but each digit ends in a smooth ROUNDED FROG TOE-PAD: absolutely NO claws, NO talons, NO nails, NO pointed fingertips anywhere. The tiger is his STYLE, not his species — he remains entirely a frog, with NO stripes, NO fur, NO whiskers and NO feline features anywhere.

His kit is a traditional Chinese martial outfit tailored to his frog anatomy, cut to show off his frog skin. He wears a SHORT-SLEEVED TANGZHUANG (Tang suit) JACKET: black silk with bold red piping, a standing MANDARIN COLLAR, and a straight center-front placket closed by a vertical column of FOUR LARGE KNOTTED FROG-BUTTON TOGGLES — each toggle chunky and clearly readable, the size of one of his eyes. The sleeves are SHORT, ending above the elbow with red-trimmed cuffs, leaving his heavy moss-green forearms bare; the jacket hem has short side slits and rests against his body. Over the jacket a broad plain cloth SASH wraps his waist, tied at the back. Below he wears loose KNEE-LENGTH training shorts, leaving his moss-green lower legs bare. Simple cloth wraps cover each forearm from wrist to below the elbow and each shin from ankle to below the knee. His big four-digit hands and bare feet show natural connected webbing — no sandals, no boots. The kit is plain: NO emblem, number, logo or readable text, and he wears NO armor, pads or helmet of any kind. He carries NOTHING — no weapon, no ball, no prop; both hands are visibly empty.

POSE AND BASING
He stands in a deep, wide TIGER-STYLE BOW STANCE facing the camera with the poised stillness of a wuxia master a heartbeat before striking: weight sunk low through strongly bent knees, torso upright, chin level. BOTH bare webbed feet are fully PLANTED on the same flat ground plane: each foot pressed FLAT along its FULL LENGTH into the ground, stance wide and rooted like a statue's — NO lifted foot, NO tiptoe, NO standing on the ball of the foot, NO raised heel, NO mid-step walking frame. Both arms present open tiger-claw hands toward the camera, one leading at shoulder height, one chambered lower near the sash, elbows bent, both arms wholly within the image plane. No limb crosses, merges into or hides behind the torso.

PRINT-SAFE FINISH
Treat every feature as geometry for a roughly 35 mm-tall resin figure: bold planes, thick connected forms, deep relief, no hairline detail. The frog-button toggles are oversized and sculptural; the jacket hem, side slits, cuffs and sash knot are thickened and rest against the body; webbed digits stay connected; the throat and chin stay clear of clutter. No base, no text, no thin spike, no free-floating strap, no transparent material.

How the round is judged

Jawaknight picks the base render, with Dimitri. Things worth weighing while looking:

  • Tangzhuang construction: mandarin collar, a readable column of chunky frog buttons, short sleeves, sash over the jacket, knee shorts — does it read as Jawa's Tang suit, or drift back toward a karate gi?
  • Frog pads: fingertips must be rounded pads, no talons. A render that is otherwise the winner but has claws is still viable — Dimitri has already accepted a claws→pads edit pass as workable — so score it "winner-with-edit" rather than rejecting outright. Same for a lifted heel: an edit pass is the accepted fallback.
  • Mass: the anthro frame must stay HEAVY — if a render slims Tiger into an acrobat it fails the brief regardless of wardrobe.

Machine checks run in two explicit lanes:

  • FAST: a fresh-context crop audit runs as each image lands, checking the held-constant program — bare head (no headband/hat), mandarin collar and frog-button placket, short sleeves with bare forearms below, sash knotted at the back, knee shorts with bare lower legs, forearm/shin wraps, bare webbed feet, four connected webbed digits per hand ending in rounded pads (no claws/talons/nails) and both hands empty in claw guard, both feet pressed flat along their full length — no tiptoe, ball-of-foot, or raised heel, no weapon/ball/mon/number/text, smooth skin with no warts or hair, no tiger features (stripes, fur, whiskers, feline face), and a serious wuxia non-cartoon tone. Once all FAST verdicts are logged, a private review may be shared as PROVISIONAL — slow checks pending so eye-review can start.
  • SLOW: after all 6 images land, one blind_detect_round.py manifest submits every spec-blind description in a single Gemini Batch wave and every dependent comparison in a second Batch wave. Completed records append to reviews.jsonl; every blind-vs-crop disagreement gets a mandatory human look. Dimitri then appends one authoritative verdict per artifact, after both machine records, whose summary starts RECONCILED — AGREE: when the latest crop and blind judgment classes match or RECONCILED — RESOLVED: with the human resolution when they differ. The panel is rebuilt as COMPLETE only after the canonical review log contains crop audits, successful records from that exact Batch run, and later reconciliation verdicts for all and only the exact 6 planned artifact ids; a partial run, missing audit, or unresolved disagreement fails closed. The provisional panel cannot close the round, promote a keeper, or authorize downstream work.

The machines screen for misses; they do not pick the winner.

Generation (both arms via OpenRouter; live balance is fetched by the preflight; the Gemini seed below is for the SLOW-lane detector):

set -euo pipefail
T=projects/martial-frogs/tiger
mkdir -p "$T/generated" "$T/notes"

# Mandatory before the first paid Gemini call on this host (detector lane).
python3 tools/spend_preflight.py set-balance gemini AMOUNT_USD

PIDS=()
for i in 1 2 3; do
  python3 tools/compare_gen.py \
    --prompt-file "$T/prompts/r2-anthro-tangzhuang.txt" \
    --out-dir "$T/generated" --out-stem "tiger-r2" \
    --variants gptimage2 --drive-sync &
  PIDS+=($!)
  ( python3 tools/openrouter_gen.py \
      --prompt-file "$T/prompts/r2-anthro-tangzhuang.txt" \
      --out-dir "$T/generated" --out-stem "tiger-r2-riverflow25" \
      --model sourceful/riverflow-v2.5-pro --resolution 2K --aspect 2:3 \
    && bash tools/drive_sync.sh push "$T/generated" ) &
  PIDS+=($!)
done
FAIL=0
for pid in "${PIDS[@]}"; do wait "$pid" || FAIL=1; done
(( FAIL == 0 )) || { echo "a generation arm failed" >&2; exit 1; }

# Exact 6-artifact detector manifest, enumerated independently of the scan.
EXPECTED="$T/notes/r2-blind-expected.txt"
ACTUAL="$T/notes/r2-blind-actual.txt"
MANIFEST="$T/notes/r2-blind-manifest.json"
for model in gptimage2 riverflow25; do
  for n in 1 2 3; do printf 'tiger-r2-%s-%s\n' "$model" "$n"; done
done | sort > "$EXPECTED"

find "$T/generated" -maxdepth 1 -type f \
  \( -name 'tiger-r2-gptimage2-*.png' -o -name 'tiger-r2-gptimage2-*.jpg' -o \
     -name 'tiger-r2-gptimage2-*.jpeg' -o -name 'tiger-r2-gptimage2-*.webp' -o \
     -name 'tiger-r2-riverflow25-*.png' -o -name 'tiger-r2-riverflow25-*.jpg' -o \
     -name 'tiger-r2-riverflow25-*.jpeg' -o -name 'tiger-r2-riverflow25-*.webp' \) \
  -printf '%f\n' | sed -E 's/\.(png|jpe?g|webp)$//' | sort > "$ACTUAL"
diff -u "$EXPECTED" "$ACTUAL"

ITEMS=()
while IFS= read -r artifact; do
  mapfile -t matches < <(find "$T/generated" -maxdepth 1 -type f \
    \( -name "$artifact.png" -o -name "$artifact.jpg" -o \
       -name "$artifact.jpeg" -o -name "$artifact.webp" \) -print)
  (( ${#matches[@]} == 1 )) || {
    echo "expected exactly one supported image for $artifact" >&2
    exit 1
  }
  ITEMS+=("$(jq -cn --arg artifact "$artifact" --arg image "${matches[0]}" \
    '{artifact: $artifact, image: $image}')")
done < "$EXPECTED"
printf '%s\n' "${ITEMS[@]}" | jq -s '{items: .}' > "$MANIFEST"
diff -u "$EXPECTED" <(jq -r '.items[].artifact' "$MANIFEST" | sort)

# SLOW lane: one two-wave Batch run; re-running resumes, never resubmits.
DETECT_LOG="$T/notes/r2-blind-detect.log"
if [[ -s "$DETECT_LOG" ]]; then
  DETECT_RUN="$(sed -n 's/^batch run: //p' "$DETECT_LOG" | head -n 1)"
  test -n "$DETECT_RUN"
  python3 tools/blind_detect_round.py --resume-run "$DETECT_RUN"
else
  python3 tools/blind_detect_round.py \
    --manifest "$MANIFEST" --spec-file "$T/spec.md" \
    --reviews "$T/reviews.jsonl" 2> >(tee "$DETECT_LOG" >&2)
  DETECT_RUN="$(sed -n 's/^batch run: //p' "$DETECT_LOG" | head -n 1)"
  test -n "$DETECT_RUN"
fi
