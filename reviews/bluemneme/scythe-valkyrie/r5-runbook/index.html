Documented technique limitations

Recorded per Dimitri's runbook-stage review (2026-08-15), so future rounds inherit them with the mechanisms:

  • Blank-and-restore cannot hide everything that telegraphs the old geometry. r4's blank under-covered the wing; the first r5 shaped blank left faint blade remnants at protect boundaries and left the head's blade socket exposed, telegraphing the old emergence angle. The revised inputs blank the socket (trimmed head-core protects) and use a tighter feather, and the restore prompts ask for a freshly formed socket — but soft residue at protected-figure boundaries is inherent to the technique.
  • A shaped blank is itself a shape cue. Blanking in a blade-shaped region invites the model to refill that same footprint with a blade instead of moving it. D′x (one large rectangle) removes the cue at the cost of a much larger restored area; D′xg adds drawn geometry back deliberately, as a guide rather than a leak.
  • A drawn straight-haft target cannot pass through everything the bowed haft touched. W1's r4-diagram axis (the counterweight-to-head chord) runs ~180 px right of her current upper grip, so straightening along it would have required re-posing her hand and arm — off-proportion-arm and ghost-hand territory. Per Dimitri's review the target is instead re-angled: the new axis pivots at the head collar and passes through her UNCHANGED grip, so no re-pose is asked anywhere (G's drawn line and the A/B text asks all describe it); the accepted trade-off is a slightly steeper, shorter upper haft with the counterweight nearer the grip. Hands keep dedicated FAST/blind checks. W2's axis line was drawn offset from its already-straight haft and is removed from its guide entirely.
  • An approved shape diagram is not automatically anchored — or oriented — correctly. The r4 arc-B diagrams drew the profile-B launch at the head COLLAR; on the actual golds the blade attaches at a socket ~150–255 px lower, so the first guides floated above the hafts. And a diagram's pixels bake in the geometry they were drawn against: after W1's axis re-angle, the extracted silhouette no longer launched perpendicular to the new haft. W2's guide is re-anchored by shift; W1's is synthesized parametrically from the approved profile-B numbers (launch perpendicular to the current axis, attachment chord parallel to it). Lesson for future guide rounds: verify a diagram's anchor and orientation against the exact base, and prefer regenerating from the approved parameters when a dependent element changed.
  • Blank coverage is a human call, with a tool — and that call was made. The computed masks took two review passes and still left slivers, so Dimitri painted clean masks of both blades through the preloaded private mask panels (PR #552, merged) on 2026-08-16. def marks(path):
    a = np.asarray(Image.open(path).convert('RGB')).astype(int)

    m = ((b - r > 50) & (g - r > 25) & (b > 90)) \
      | ((r > 195) & (g > 195) & (b > 195)) \
