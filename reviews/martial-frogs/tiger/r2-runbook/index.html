Goal

Round 1's feedback set the body-plan direction — anthro frame, heavyweight mass, recorded PRELIMINARY with Jawaknight's formal pick still open (this round's results panel re-asks it with real renders) — and the wardrobe question answered itself in the Discord feedback: Tiger and the linos wear traditional Chinese tangzhuang, not gi-and-headband training wear. This round generates the first concepts of the working design contract (spec v2) and asks Jawaknight and Dimitri to pick ONE render as the lineage base that carries into pose/camera refinement and the turnaround. It is a fresh text-only round: no r1 artifact is consumed, so nothing here depends on the still-open r1 reconciliations.

The 2026-08-06 amendment adds a piggybacked prompt-length experiment (Dimitri's ask): our prompts only ever grow — every defect earns another constraint or negative — and we have never measured whether the added text still helps or has started to hurt. Two mechanisms make harm plausible: attention dilution (many simultaneous constraints each get less weight) and negation leakage (the full prompt names claws, talons, stripes, headbands and hats in order to forbid them, and image models sometimes render mentioned-but-negated concepts — r1 drew literal claws). All three prompt tiers target the SAME resolved design, so any roll from any arm can still win the base pick; the experiment rides along at zero extra decision cost.

The choices on the table

Every arm targets the SAME resolved design; the primary choice being made is which model's rendering becomes the lineage base. The model panel is narrowed to the two models whose r1 anthro renders led both audit lanes — gptimage2 (the only r1 artifact both machine lanes rated FIXABLE) and riverflow25 — a narrowing Jawaknight confirmed on Discord 2026-08-05 ("sounds good" to gptimage2 + riverflow only). Three gptimage2 rolls per tier, because feature defects are a stochastic lottery and its rolls are cheap; riverflow25 runs two per tier since its per-call price is roughly double (second amendment).

The amendment adds a second, orthogonal axis: THREE prompt tiers of the same design at ~831 / ~460 / ~150 words. Full is the working prompt with every constraint and negative. Compressed keeps the ENTIRE constraint set at roughly half the words — it isolates pure verbosity. Minimal keeps only the essence (identity, kit summary, stance, tone; no counts, no negatives, no print-safe block) — it tests whether the model's own priors beat our full specification.

Models and shared input conventions: both models run all three tiers, text-only, no reference images; riverflow25 at 2K with --aspect 2:3.

OUTPUT AND CAMERA
A photorealistic full-body studio character render designed specifically as production concept art for a 32 mm-scale resin fantasy-football miniature, in the visual language of a premium collectible sports sculpt. Show one figure only, straight-on FRONT view, centered on a plain neutral gray studio background with even soft lighting. Use a near-orthographic, very-long-lens studio view with minimal foreshortening so head, torso, both arms, both hands, legs and feet can be compared as a proportion sheet. The entire body and both feet are visible with space around them. This is character concept art, not a photograph of a toy: no base, pedestal, scenic ground, labels or presentation card.

FROG BODY PLAN — ANTHRO HEAVYWEIGHT
Build him as an ANTHRO ATHLETE FROG, roughly FIVE to FIVE-AND-A-HALF heads tall — an upright humanoid read. His frame, stance and limb proportions are those of a HEAVYWEIGHT human martial artist: wide shoulders, deep chest, thick powerful limbs — the densest, most powerful member of his squad, never lean or wiry. His head is an unmistakable frog head with dome eyes and a wide mouth on a short thick neck, and his hands and feet are big webbed frog hands and feet on long powerful frog legs. He reads first as a powerful humanoid athlete and second as a frog.

HELD-CONSTANT CHARACTER PROGRAM
TIGER is a FROG — a martial-arts fantasy-football lineman from an ancient kung-fu temple, rendered as the hero of a serious WUXIA epic: the disciplined gravitas, coiled power and quiet menace of a wuxia film lead, an ancient-Chinese martial-world tone carried in every element. Played absolutely straight — never cartoon-cute, never comedic, never a Ninja Turtles homage. Smooth moss-green frog skin with a lighter throat and belly; no warts, no hair. His head is COMPLETELY BARE: NO headband, NO hachimaki, NO hat, hood or helmet — nothing worn on the head at all. His face is level and unsmiling, the wide frog mouth a firm closed line — never a grin, never googly eyes.

He is a master of TIGER-STYLE KUNG FU, the hard ferocious power style: every technique drives forward through open TIGER-CLAW hands. "Tiger-claw" names the HAND SHAPE ONLY — his thick webbed digits are hooked like a striking claw but each digit ends in a smooth ROUNDED FROG TOE-PAD: absolutely NO claws, NO talons, NO nails, NO pointed fingertips anywhere. The tiger is his STYLE, not his species — he remains entirely a frog, with NO stripes, NO fur, NO whiskers and NO feline features anywhere.

His kit is a traditional Chinese martial outfit tailored to his frog anatomy, cut to show off his frog skin. He wears a SHORT-SLEEVED TANGZHUANG (Tang suit) JACKET: black silk with bold red piping, a standing MANDARIN COLLAR, and a straight center-front placket closed by a vertical column of exactly FOUR LARGE KNOTTED FROG-BUTTON TOGGLES — each toggle chunky and clearly readable, the size of one of his eyes. The sleeves are SHORT, ending above the elbow with red-trimmed cuffs, showing his heavy moss-green upper arms; the jacket hem has short side slits and rests against his body. Over the jacket a broad plain cloth SASH wraps his waist, tied at the back. Below he wears loose KNEE-LENGTH training shorts. Simple cloth wraps cover each LOWER forearm from the wrist up and each shin from the ankle up, each wrap stopping short of the elbow or knee so a clear band of moss-green skin shows between wrap and sleeve cuff on each arm, and at each knee between shorts hem and shin wrap. His big four-digit hands and bare feet show natural connected webbing — no sandals, no boots. The kit is plain: NO emblem, number, logo or readable text, and he wears NO armor, pads or helmet of any kind. He carries NOTHING — no weapon, no ball, no prop; both hands are visibly empty.

POSE AND BASING
He stands in a deep, wide TIGER-STYLE BOW STANCE facing the camera with the poised stillness of a wuxia master a heartbeat before striking: weight sunk low through strongly bent knees, torso upright, chin level. BOTH bare webbed feet are fully PLANTED on the same flat ground plane: each foot pressed FLAT along its FULL LENGTH into the ground, stance wide and rooted like a statue's — NO lifted foot, NO tiptoe, NO standing on the ball of the foot, NO raised heel, NO mid-step walking frame. Both arms present open tiger-claw hands toward the camera, one leading at shoulder height, one chambered lower near the sash, elbows bent, both arms wholly within the image plane. No limb crosses, merges into or hides behind the torso.

PRINT-SAFE FINISH
Treat every feature as geometry for a roughly 35 mm-tall resin figure: bold planes, thick connected forms, deep relief, no hairline detail. The frog-button toggles are oversized and sculptural; the jacket hem, side slits, cuffs and sash knot are thickened and rest against the body; webbed digits stay connected; the throat and chin stay clear of clutter. No base, no text, no thin spike, no free-floating strap, no transparent material.

How the round is judged

Jawaknight picks the base render, with Dimitri. Things worth weighing while looking:

  • Tangzhuang construction: mandarin collar, a readable column of chunky frog buttons, short sleeves, sash over the jacket, knee shorts — does it read as Jawa's Tang suit, or drift back toward a karate gi?
  • Frog pads: fingertips must be rounded pads, no talons. A render that is otherwise the winner but has claws is still viable — Dimitri has already accepted a claws→pads edit pass as workable — so score it "winner-with-edit" rather than rejecting outright. Same for a lifted heel: an edit pass is the accepted fallback.
  • Mass: the anthro frame must stay HEAVY — if a render slims Tiger into an acrobat it fails the brief regardless of wardrobe.

Machine checks run in two explicit lanes:

  • FAST: a fresh-context crop audit runs as each image lands, checking the held-constant program — bare head (no headband/hat), mandarin collar and a placket column of exactly four large frog-button toggles, short sleeves with a visible band of moss-green skin between each sleeve cuff and forearm wrap, sash worn over the jacket with no knot visible at the front or sides and no hanging sash tails (the back knot itself is a rear-view condition, verified at turnaround time — a front view cannot prove it), knee shorts with moss-green skin showing at the knees above the shin wraps, lower-forearm and shin wraps, bare webbed feet, four connected webbed digits per hand ending in rounded pads (no claws/talons/nails) and both hands empty in claw guard, both feet pressed flat along their full length — no tiptoe, ball-of-foot, or raised heel, no weapon/ball/mon/number/text, smooth skin with no warts or hair, no tiger features (stripes, fur, whiskers, feline face), and a serious wuxia non-cartoon tone. The same program is checked for ALL 15 images regardless of prompt tier: for the minimal arm, a violation of a constraint its prompt deliberately dropped is recorded as experiment DATA (prior-vs-spec divergence), not as a defect to fix — but it still disqualifies that roll from being picked as the lineage base uncorrected. Once all FAST verdicts are logged, a private review may be shared as PROVISIONAL — slow checks pending so eye-review can start.
  • SLOW: after all 15 images land, one blind_detect_round.py manifest submits every spec-blind description in a single Gemini Batch wave and every dependent comparison in a second Batch wave. Completed records append to reviews.jsonl; every blind-vs-crop disagreement gets a mandatory human look. Dimitri then appends one authoritative verdict per artifact, after both machine records, whose summary starts RECONCILED — AGREE: when the latest crop and blind judgment classes match or RECONCILED — RESOLVED: with the human resolution when they differ. The panel is rebuilt as COMPLETE only after the canonical review log contains crop audits, successful records from that exact Batch run, and later reconciliation verdicts for all and only the exact 15 planned artifact ids; a partial run, missing audit, or unresolved disagreement fails closed. The provisional panel cannot close the round, promote a keeper, or authorize downstream work.

The machines screen for misses; they do not pick the winner.

Scoring the prompt-length experiment. The blind detector is the experiment's instrument: it never sees the prompt, so its per-attribute ok/warn/bad counts are comparable across arms without bias toward any tier. At round close, tabulate per-arm compliance two ways — (a) all spec attributes, and (b) for the minimal arm, ONLY the constraints its prompt kept (identity, kit summary, stance, tone) — plus the human eye-read of holistic quality/coherence per arm. n=3 (gptimage2) / n=2 (riverflow25) per cell is a directional read, not proof: a clear effect either way is actionable, and a wash means verbosity is not the problem. The finding — including a null result — is recorded in docs/image-model-policy.md (per-model, since the effect may differ between gptimage2 and riverflow25) and summarized in rounds.md.

import difflib
import json
import pathlib
import sys

generated = pathlib.Path(sys.argv[1])
expected_path = pathlib.Path(sys.argv[2])
actual_path = pathlib.Path(sys.argv[3])
manifest_path = pathlib.Path(sys.argv[4])

stems = ("tiger-r2", "tiger-r2-comp", "tiger-r2-min")
rolls = {"gptimage2": 3, "riverflow25": 2}
expected = sorted(
    f"{stem}-{model}-{roll}"
    for stem in stems
    for model, count in rolls.items()
    for roll in range(1, count + 1)
)

found = {}
for path in generated.iterdir():
    if not path.is_file() or path.suffix.lower() not in {
        ".png", ".jpg", ".jpeg", ".webp"
    }:
        continue
    if path.stem.startswith(tuple(
        f"{stem}-{model}-" for stem in stems for model in rolls
    )):
        found.setdefault(path.stem, []).append(path)

actual = sorted(found)
expected_path.write_text("\n".join(expected) + "\n", encoding="utf-8")
actual_path.write_text("\n".join(actual) + "\n", encoding="utf-8")

if actual != expected:
    sys.stderr.writelines(
        difflib.unified_diff(
            [f"{artifact}\n" for artifact in expected],
            [f"{artifact}\n" for artifact in actual],
            fromfile=str(expected_path),
            tofile=str(actual_path),
        )
    )
    raise SystemExit("generated artifacts do not match the exact r2 plan")

duplicates = {
    artifact: paths
    for artifact, paths in found.items()
    if len(paths) != 1
}
if duplicates:
    for artifact, paths in sorted(duplicates.items()):
        print(
            f"{artifact}: expected one image, found {len(paths)}",
            file=sys.stderr,
        )
    raise SystemExit("duplicate artifact extensions found")

manifest = {
    "items": [
        {"artifact": artifact, "image": str(found[artifact][0])}
        for artifact in expected
    ]
}
manifest_path.write_text(
    json.dumps(manifest, indent=2) + "\n",
    encoding="utf-8",
)
PY
