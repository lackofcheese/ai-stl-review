Goal

Get the heroic proportion contract to actually land, on the signature Gorgon. Round 1 proved two things: numeric heads-tall language suppresses the very head size it names, and the front-view proportion sheet costs us the pose that made Gorgon *Gorgon*. This round returns to the v1 golds' side-on stiff-arm stride and attacks proportions qualitatively — an explicitly oversized head, oversized hands and boots, chunky joints — while exaggerating every material boundary so the printed, unpainted figure reads (Jeremy's v1 test print: cyber arms, bare flesh and the covered leg were indistinguishable; all belts and studs need to be thicker and bolder).

The choices on the table

Both arms share the same target: the r6 gold's camera, pose, hair drape and nape implant, rebuilt to heroic proportions with print-readable exaggeration. They differ in how they get there.

How the round is judged

Dimitri, Jason and Jeremy make the call. Judge in this order:

  1. Head first. Does the head finally read oversized against the body — never a small head on a big frame? This is the axis R1 failed.
  2. Signature fidelity. Is this still the golds' Gorgon — camera, stride, stiff-arm, hair-forward drape, nape implant, kit asymmetry?
  3. Print readability at thumbnail. Squint test: do chrome, flesh and covered cloth separate by geometry alone? Are the studs one big obvious row, and the belts thick enough to survive resin?

Arm A is additionally judged for edit locality: what stayed unchanged counts as heavily as what was fixed. Machine checks run in two explicit lanes:

  • FAST: a fresh-context crop audit runs as each image lands, checking the three decision axes above plus anatomy, single-figure composition, grounded feet, kit/chirality, and arm A's edit locality. Once all FAST verdicts are logged, the private review may be shared as PROVISIONAL — slow checks pending so eye-review can start.
  • SLOW: after all 7 images land, one blind_detect_round.py manifest submits every spec-blind description in a single Gemini Batch wave and every dependent comparison in a second Batch wave. Completed records append to reviews.jsonl; every blind-vs-crop disagreement gets a mandatory human look. The same panel is rebuilt as COMPLETE only after the canonical review log contains one successful record from that Batch run for each of the exact 7 planned artifact ids. A partial detector run remains incomplete even when the coordinator exits normally. The provisional panel cannot close the round, promote a keeper, or authorize downstream work.

Machines screen; your eye decides.
