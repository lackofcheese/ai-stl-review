Scythe Valkyrie — targeted edit mechanisms

📖 Round runbook →

Round 3 tests four ways to repair the two selected wallpapers: progressively larger full-frame edit bundles, exemplar-guided blade shaping, a fixed-input sequential grip pass, and a deterministic crop-and-paste-back dagger repair. Each arm is shown next to its exact base; locality figures are navigation evidence, not an automatic gate.

ROUND CLOSED — 2026-08-12. Jake and Dimitri reviewed these candidates and picked none of them as a finished wallpaper. Two carry into round 4 as starting points to edit further: W1 · removals · roll 2, chosen by Dimitri for its wings — the clear mechanical joint along the top with the feathers hanging below it — even though the edits actually asked for on it failed; and W2 · everything · roll 2, chosen by Jake, who wants everything from it except the hand. Two design rules came out of this round and are now part of the character spec: each wing is built as a jointed mechanical limb with nothing rising above its top edge, and the scythe blade leaves the shaft at a right angle without curving round too far. The blade shape for round 4 was picked from ten drawn options. Dimitri also set aside the round's one missing automated check — the job it belonged to had already finished, so getting that result would have meant paying for a fresh run, and it would not have satisfied the round's original condition anyway. The pass/fail labels on the cards below are the machine's original notes and were never the decision.
