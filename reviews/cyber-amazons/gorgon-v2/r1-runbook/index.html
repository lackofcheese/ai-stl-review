Goal

Restart Gorgon from text as a miniature-first design. We keep the identity that Jason and Dimitri shaped — the cold mil-spec Juicer, cable-dread mane, spinal ridge, punk kit, asymmetric legs and stiff-arm — but make heroic scale a measurable body-proportion contract instead of a passing style word.

This round stops at the body recipe. It deliberately uses no original Gorgon image, so the previous long legs, small head and naturalistic hands cannot condition the new lineage.

The choices on the table

The four ratified fresh-concept defaults — flash31, gptimage2, seedream45 and grokq — render both choices. Every other character, kit, pose and camera sentence is held constant.

H1 — classic heroic1 prompt

~6.75 heads tall; head, hands and boots ~15% oversized; tall athletic pewter-sports silhouette

Text-only — no image inputs; the prompt below is the whole input.

Prompt — r1-h1-classic-heroic.txt 6 kB

projects/cyber-amazons/gorgon-v2/prompts/r1-h1-classic-heroic.txt

No images are attached. Create a brand-new text-to-image concept; do not copy any previous Gorgon artwork.

OUTPUT AND CAMERA
A photorealistic full-body studio character render designed specifically as production concept art for a 32 mm HEROIC-SCALE resin fantasy-football miniature, in the visual language of a chunky pewter sports sculpt. Show one figure only, straight-on FRONT view, centered on a plain neutral gray studio background with even soft lighting. Use a near-orthographic, very-long-lens studio view with minimal foreshortening so head, torso, both arms, both hands, legs and boots can be compared as a proportion sheet. Her entire body and both boots are visible with space around them. This is character concept art, not a photograph of a toy: no base, pedestal, scenic ground, labels or presentation card.

HEROIC PROPORTION TARGET — H1 CLASSIC HEROIC
Build her at roughly SIX-AND-THREE-QUARTERS HEADS TALL. Compared with naturalistic character art, enlarge her head, both hands and both boots by about fifteen percent. Give her the long-but-sturdy read of a classic heroic-scale pewter sports sculpt: thick neck, deep ribcage and pelvis, broad readable muscle planes, thick wrists and ankles, compact limb segments and substantial joints. Her head must not look small between her shoulders; both hands must read clearly at thumbnail size; her combat boots must feel weighty enough for a Blood Bowl miniature. She remains tall, athletic and visibly female, never chibi or squat. Absolutely no fashion-model proportions, tiny head, tiny hands, narrow wrists, long delicate legs or slender ankles.

HELD-CONSTANT CHARACTER PROGRAM
GORGON is a female cyberpunk fantasy-football BLOCKER: a corporate super-soldier washout turned cold street-team enforcer. She is one of the two biggest players on the roster, a heavyweight powerlifter with a tall V-shaped silhouette: extremely broad shoulders, a deep upper torso, thick neck, dense back and lats, compact waist and massive thighs. Her menace is disciplined stillness, not rage; high cheekbones, level unsmiling stare, never a snarl and never a pin-up.

FRONT-VIEW SIDE MAP: the VIEWER'S LEFT side contains the completely exposed cyber-shoulder, the clear head-jack temple landing zone, the dread drape, the continuous covered leg and the in-plane stiff-arm. The VIEWER'S RIGHT side contains the single shoulder pad, the bare garter leg and the hanging fist. This side map is absolute for the whole image: do not swap or mirror it.

Both arms are complete military-grade cyberarms from shoulder to fingertips: symmetrical gunmetal armored volumes, slightly oversized, with broad beveled plates and DEEP recessed seams that will survive a small resin print. They are precise mil-spec grafts, never skinny android limbs and never exposed industrial hydraulics. Both hands are deliberately oversized heroic-scale chrome hands, each with exactly four thick fingers and one thumb.

Both temples are clean-shaved with NO socket, jewel, port or forehead mark. The entire temple on the clear-zone/dread-drape side, from brow to above the ear, stays completely free of hair so a head-jack can be added there later; the mane begins behind that landing zone and never rises across it. A dense mane of thumb-thick cable-dreadlocks sweeps straight back and rests as one connected printable mass over the exposed-shoulder side. The locks have blunt connected ends: no thin tips, loops or floating strands. Behind the mane, a bold segmented vertebral ridge runs from the back of her head to mid-back; its large plates are visible only where the front camera naturally permits, never as spikes.

Her kit is scuffed anti-corporate cyberpunk sports armor in matte black and gunmetal with restrained hot-magenta accents: a compression playing suit, broad armor planes, chunky low-relief studs and a torn-off corporate chest patch with no readable lettering. ONE layered angular fantasy-football shoulder pad sits on the pad side only. The opposite stiff-arm side is the completely exposed rounded cyber-shoulder: NO second pad, cap, strap or mirrored plate. No number, team emblem, animal logo, word or readable graffiti anywhere.

Her leg asymmetry is explicit. The pad-side leg is bare muscular flesh from mid-thigh to the boot and wears one wide studded leather garter plus a strapped leather knee pad. The stiff-arm-side leg is covered by ONE continuous unbroken leather-and-armor legging from the hip crease all the way into the boot, with a hard armored knee and shin plate: no bare-thigh gap, no garter, no rip, no cutout. Both feet wear oversized buckled fantasy-football combat boots with broad toe boxes and thick lugged soles, never sneakers.

POSE AND BASING
She advances in a shallow, controlled stagger with BOTH feet firmly reaching the same ground plane and two generous glue patches. The front boot is flat from heel through ball; the rear boot is also visibly grounded through heel and ball. No lifted foot, tiptoe or long running stride. Torso upright and square, chin level. The stiff-arm-side cyberarm extends diagonally outward at shoulder height, about FORTY DEGREES away from the torso and wholly within the image plane, elbow slightly bent; it does NOT point toward the camera. Its open palm faces the camera so all four joined fingers and the separate thumb are clearly readable without shortening the arm. The pad-side cyberarm hangs beside her body with a loose empty fist. Both hands are empty. No limb crosses, merges into or hides behind the torso.

PRINT-SAFE FINISH
Treat every feature as geometry for a 37 mm-tall resin figure: bold planes, thick connected forms, deep relief, no hairline seams. Cyberarm panel lines are engraved at least 0.15 mm at final scale. No weapon, ball, other person, jungle motif, cape, antenna, free cable, blade, thin spike, transparent material, base or text.

How the round is judged

Jason and Jeremy make the call, with Dimitri: does this body read like a blocker who belongs beside the rest of the team?

Compare H1 against H2 within each model — the four models draw faces, kit and lighting quite differently, so an H1 from one against an H2 from another compares house styles, not proportions.

Machine checks run on every image before you see them, but treat them as experimental and still in development: a blind detector, a fresh-context crop audit, and a set of landmark measurements testing whether H2 really did come back heavier than H1 (the exact metrics are in the technical plan). They exist to catch obvious misses — a missing shoulder pad, three fingers, a mirrored side map — and to flag where the two machine readers disagree with each other. They do not pick a winner, and your call overrides them.

One machine result is worth acting on: if the measurements say H1 and H2 barely differ, that is a real finding rather than a tie to break — better to widen the bracket in a later round than to pick one and pretend.

What we need from you

Pick H1, H2 or a blend as the v2 proportion contract, then pick one concept render as the lineage base — or record no measurable separation/reject all 8 with a clearer body target. That choice greenlights a separate r2 pose/camera refinement; no turnaround or Meshy spend is bundled into this round.

Technical plan — models, prompts, commands (pipeline detail)

What changed going in (spec / prompt / process deltas)

  • New sibling project projects/cyber-amazons/gorgon-v2/; the original gorgon/ history and recommended r12 print-master candidate remain untouched.
  • spec.md separates miniature proportions from body mass and records the physical team-standard chirality. It carries forward only human-confirmed Gorgon identity, print, basing, mirror-cheap and turnaround rules.
  • The two arms deliberately bracket a wide 6.75-head versus 5.75-head range; paired landmark ratios, not pooled impressions, determine whether that treatment survives each model attractor.
  • The stiff-arm remains lineage-defining but sits about 40° laterally in the image plane instead of pointing at the camera, so its length and hand scale remain measurable.
  • The deferred head-jack's right-temple clearance and the ≥0.15 mm cyberarm panel-line floor are restored from the v1 evidence base.
  • Two exact prompt files differ only in the named heroic-proportion paragraph. Their HELD-CONSTANT CHARACTER PROGRAM through PRINT-SAFE FINISH text is byte-identical.
  • Both calls explicitly declare zero attached images. This is a clean text lineage, not img2img repair.
  • Issue #200's durability gap is fixed by merged PR #206. Both generation arms require --drive-sync, which pushes each completed cell before slower sibling cells finish and includes WebP among the synced binary formats.
  • The explicit panel follows the ratified 2026-08-02 image policy: flash31,gptimage2,seedream45,grokq. pro2k is not needed as a diagnosed Flash fallback, and retired/suspended Cyber-Amazons models stay excluded.
  • riverflow25 remains a supported, project-proven Gorgon specialist, but this body-proportion round does not need a fifth model attractor. Its historical aspect-locked limitation is not carried forward: the public OpenRouter image catalog, checked 2026-08-02, exposes 2K plus aspect_ratio, including 2:3, for sourceful/riverflow-v2.5-pro. A later runbook that names Riverflow must recheck the live schema and pass explicit --aspect 2:3 for this full-body portrait framing rather than relying on auto.
  • This round does not add the head jack, jersey number or Jaguar emblem; those remain standalone late assets so mirrors are recoverable and micro-hardware is not judged from a full-body render.

The experimental machine checks (screening only, not a gate)

Every output gets the standard blind-detect pass plus a fresh-context crop audit. Both are screens for the human panel, and this round's landmark measurement is itself under development — it is being trialled here, not trusted. Disagreement between the blind detector and the crop audit is highlighted for the humans rather than resolved by score.

H1 and H2 are compared only as four within-model pairs, never as two pooled groups. On each near-orthographic, very-long-lens front image, the audit records pixel landmarks for crown, chin and sole; shoulder endpoints; the left/right bounds of the head and face; the hanging fist and in-plane open-hand bounding boxes; the widest boot, calf, wrist, forearm and ankle spans; and the stiff-arm shoulder, elbow, wrist and middle-fingertip centers. Record heads-tall, head/shoulder, hand/face, boot/calf, wrist/forearm and ankle/calf ratios as diagnostics.

The paired reading uses seven predeclared contract metrics, each normalized to crown-to-sole height except heads-tall: heads-tall and stiff-arm polyline length (shoulder–elbow–wrist–fingertip) must decrease from H1 to H2; shoulder breadth, mean hand maximum dimension, mean boot width, mean wrist width and mean ankle width must increase. A relative change smaller than 5% in either direction is neutral annotation/model noise. One model pair counts as heavy-heroic movement only when at least four of the seven metrics cross 5% in their stated direction, and H2 must do that in at least three of the four model pairs. Otherwise the honest machine result is no measurable separation: report it as such for the human panel — never pool model styles or change the metric set after seeing the outputs to manufacture one.

The audit also judges torso depth and whether the result still reads tall, femme and athletic rather than chibi or male-coded, then checks exactly one pad, both cyberarms, both distinct legs, two grounded feet, all digits, empty hands, connected dreads, a clear anatomical-right-temple landing zone on the VIEWER'S LEFT side, and absence of text/base.
