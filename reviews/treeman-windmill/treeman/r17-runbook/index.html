Meshes — Drive push the moment each roll lands, unit-neutral trimesh validation (watertightness, components, bounds), turnaround + base-plane renders, then a per-arm fresh-context comparative review against the source photographs as gold with the sails first. The round's headline deliverable is the cross-arm tally: per arm, the fraction of rolls with (a) all four sails straight, (b) spar/board construction surviving, (c) a watertight single shell, (d) no detached/ghost rotor geometry. 5–6 rolls per arm separates gross rate differences; subtle ones will stay noisy and are not over-read.

SLOW: one blind_detect_round.py two-wave Batch manifest over all eight images, C1–C9 detectors (spec v2, unchanged) on the four clay sheets, and mandatory blind-vs-crop reconciliation.
