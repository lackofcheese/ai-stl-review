48 rolls total — 4 choices, 12 + 12 + 12 + 12

Four cases × 12 rolls = 48 Meshy calls at 20 credits each = the full 960-credit cap. The doubled depth uses more of the expiring balance while keeping the four-way comparison balanced, and gives each arm a stronger read on Meshy's stochastic sail-defect rate. Deliberate exclusions from the r17 pre-announcement, on the r17 evidence: the full-rear arm is dropped (three of the four synthesized rear views failed on camera — there is only one usable new azimuth, not a rear set); the clay orig-flash31 sheet is excluded (audited FIXABLE: a flared/smeared sail and a depth error that draws a blade in front of the torso — poison for reconstruction); and the 225° view is used as-is, through-holes unfilled — the same physical holes already ride in the r16 rear that every r17 arm consumed. A fill pass would be a new paid image cell, while the existing pixel-level crop audit and Dimitri's eye already answer this round's input question more directly. If its meshes come back holed at the rotor, the fill pass becomes an explicit r19+ choice rather than a silent addition here.
